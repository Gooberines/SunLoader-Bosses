# Sun Loader Bosses

Custom bosses for **Just Shapes & Beats**, shared from inside the game with
[Sun Loader](https://github.com/Gooberines/SunLoader).

Each [issue](../../issues) here is one boss. Its 👍 / 👎 reactions are its rating, and its comments are where you can
talk about it.

## Playing them

In the game, open the **Bosses** menu (main menu, or **F4**) → **Online**. Download any boss, then play it from
**My bosses**. You don't need a GitHub account to browse or download.

## Sharing yours

In **My bosses**, press **Share** on a boss. You sign in with GitHub once. The Sun Loader app can only post and react to
issues in this repository, nothing else on your account.

Please post bosses through the game, so they keep the format Sun Loader reads. Bosses contain attack data only,
never code or song files.

## Rules

Be nice. Don't post other people's bosses as your own. Spam and offensive posts get closed, and their authors may be
blocked.
